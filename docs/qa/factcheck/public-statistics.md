# Fact-check — public-statistics
Checked: 207 statements — about 85 sentence-level statements on `/comprendre/depenses/` and `/comprendre/europe/` (lede, 3 stat tiles, unit notes, chart titles/subtitles/readouts in all 4 views of the Europe chart, treemap summary, 100 € grid, source and "écarts" lists, callout, disclosure, per-foyer / per-day / per-inhabitant reframings, ranks), 90 COFOG table rows (value, share, € per foyer per year / per day / per inhabitant, i.e. 450 numbers) and 32 Europe table rows (6 values each) — plus the datasets `depenses-cofog.json` and `europe.json` and all 91 `donnee` facts of `facts.json` (23 Eurostat, 20 Insee, 10 DGAFP, 4 Légifrance, 1 Code du travail, 27 PLF-annex / workbook, 6 amendment-cited). Verified at the official source: the 23 Eurostat, 20 Insee, 10 DGAFP and the Code du travail facts, and two PLF 2026 figures (465 niches, 89,4 Md€). Only partly verifiable: the 4 Légifrance facts (site blocked). Not re-read at source: the other PLF-annex / workbook facts (CSG-CRDS, taxes affectées, ETPT, TVA…) and the 6 figures quoted inside amendments (see V5, V6, V8). Errors: 6 (blocker 0 / major 1 / minor 5). À vérifier: 11.

Frozen build used for the rendered text: `scratchpad/factcheck-dist/comprendre/{depenses,europe}/index.html`, plus Playwright on the Europe chart to read the "Déficit" and "Revenu médian" (€ and SPA) views, which are not in the static HTML.
