# Fact-check — questionnaire-situations-glossary

Checked: about 560 statements. That is the 19 questions of `/questionnaire/` (about 215: prompt, reference answer, unit, every explanation sentence, caveat, source chip, tier, « N fois plus » ratio, closing-summary rule), the 18 fiches of `/comprendre/situations/` (about 240: header, tagline, 71 gains, 20 pertes, 24 « comme chacun », 8 sub-items, 49 amendment links, 6 group labels) and the 53 glossary entries (about 105 claims).
Files and pages covered: `site/src/lib/questionnaire.ts`, `questionnaire-model.ts`, `site/src/components/questionnaire/Questionnaire.tsx`, rendered `questionnaire/index.html` (JS island and `<noscript>`), `site/src/lib/situations.ts`, `site/src/pages/comprendre/situations.astro`, rendered `comprendre/situations/index.html`, `site/src/data/fiches.json`, `site/src/data/glossaire.json`, `site/src/data/amendements.json`.

Errors: 11 (blocker 1 / major 1 / minor 9). À vérifier: 9.

Method:
- Every fiche item was compared programmatically with `research/pages/fiches__*.md`, and then with the rendered page text.
- Every questionnaire number was recomputed from `facts.json` and the CSVs.
- The 81 amendment PDF page anchors were re-derived from the form-feed positions in `research/pages/amendements__*.md`.
- Insee, Eurostat and DGAFP figures were checked against the primary source where reachable.

Reachability from the sandbox:
- Eurostat API: OK.
- DGAFP PDFs: OK.
- Assemblée nationale PDFs: OK.
- Insee: HTTP 500 on every page and file. The Insee COFOG 2024 table was therefore checked through Eurostat `gov_10a_exp`, which republishes the same Insee data (France, 2024, updated 2026-10-21).
- Légifrance: 403. The word counts of `/questionnaire/` Q11 could not be checked at the source.
