## Checked for changes between October 2026 and 21 November 2026

- **Décret of 10/11/2026** (JO): reduces employer relief for branches whose minima are below the SMIC. It applies from 2027, so 2026 is unchanged (selectra.info 10/11/2026).
- **PLFSS 2027**: rejected by the Assemblée at first reading (23/10/2026). The PLF 2027 is still in debate. Nothing enacted changes the 2026 parameters. Announced measures (fuel bonus up to 1 000 €, overtime ceiling 10 000 €, 2027 APL freeze) are not law.
- **AF age majoration**: still 18 for children born from 1 Mar 2012 (décret 2026-138). Neither the Senate's opposition nor a written-question answer about restoring 14 produced an adopted text.
- **No off-cycle uprating** of RSA, PA, AAH or BMAF after 1 Apr 2026 (searched). APL was revalued on 1 Oct 2026, as the engine has it.
