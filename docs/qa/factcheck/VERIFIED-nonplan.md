# Adversarial verification — findings that do not depend on the plan's text

Verifier pass on 2026-11-21. Scope: current-law, public-statistics and engine/UI findings from the reports in `docs/qa/factcheck/`. Findings that depend on the text of the amendments, fiches or workbook are left to the re-diff agent. **Method.** Each finding was re-checked against a primary source:
- service-public.gouv.fr: F39152, F345, F19778, F14860, F2300, F35063, F1563;
- code.travail.gouv.fr (10/07/2026) and BOFiP BOI-IR-LIQ-20-20-40 §60–90;

The engine was re-run with a script: scratchpad `vz/round.ts`, run with `node --experimental-strip-types`, over all 47 `CAS_TYPES` and `apl()`. caf.fr was in maintenance and Légifrance was unreachable.
