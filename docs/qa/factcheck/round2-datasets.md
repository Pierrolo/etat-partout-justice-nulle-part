## Resolution of the three open items (item 4)

1. **The « 41 » TVA niches « supprimée au 1er juillet 2027 »** (really 38 + 3 extinct; 5 overseas have their own plan). **Not what the text says.** See M1a: 22 are named by III bis (supprimées 1 Jul 2027), 6 exemptions fall under the general rule (1 Jul 2027), 6 rates are not named (kept by II 4°; earliest 1 Jul 2028 via n° 21), 2 partial, 2 overseas excluded. The authors' exposés (Am. 1, Am. 21) announce all of them for 1 Jul 2027, so show it as a text/exposé conflict.
2. **60203 and 120124 « En 3 ans » but not named in IV.E.** Both art. 1647-00 bis and art. 81 A are cited nowhere in Am. 1. IV.E is defined by nature (« dispositions relatives aux activités agricoles, aux services à la personne et à la garde des jeunes enfants, **au nombre desquelles** celles que le III abroge à ses … »), the numbers are examples. **60203** (dégrèvement jeunes agriculteurs, TFNB) is agricultural: `trois-ans`, keep, flag inferred. **120124** is not: `supprimee`, IV.A (income tax due for 2026), cost 369 M€ (M4).
3. **110203 (crédit d'impôt garde d'enfants < 6 ans, art. 200 quater B, 1 737 M€).** Keep `trois-ans` (IV.E: one third off for 2027, two thirds for 2028, out from 2029) with `inferred: true`. Supports: IV.E names « la garde des jeunes enfants » and 150° (« 23° bis du II de la section V ») ; the sequence 149° = art. 200 quater A, 150° = 23° bis, 151° = 23° ter fits art. 200 quater A, B, C ; Am. 28 VII does not abrogate art. 200 quater B, so the workbook's « Fusion CI IR » is not supported. The numbering 23° bis = 200 quater B cannot be proven here (Légifrance returns HTTP 403 from this sandbox).
